### LEAP (founded in Australia; the incumbent for AU small firms)

**AI features**
- **LawY:** AI legal Q&A with optional lawyer verification.
- **Matter AI:** answers questions across all of a matter's documents and emails, including OCR of scans and handwriting (May 2024).
- <https://www.leaplegalsoftware.com/au/features/matterai>

**Complaints**
- Capterra AU rating 4.3/5 from 541 reviews.
- Recurring themes: price rises on 3-year renewals, restrictive termination, hard to get data out.
- <https://www.capterra.com.au/reviews/147413/leap>

**API:** no official public API. Third parties sell "unofficial" reverse-engineered APIs (Supergood) and read-mostly integrations (Unified.to). <https://supergood.ai/docs/leap-api>

### Smokeball (US/AU; strong in AU small firms)

**Archie AI**
- Launched Jul 2024. A next-generation agentic version shipped Feb–Mar 2026. <https://lawnext.com/2026/07/two-years-after-launching-its-ai-assistant-archie-smokeball-rolls-out-the-next-generation-built-on-agentic-ai-and-embedded-in-word-and-outlook.html>

## 4. Gaps (summary; see STRATEGY §3)

1. Medicare, Centrelink and NDIS clearances are manual and not built into any practice management system.
2. WA scheme logic (ICWA, the WCIMA 2023, r 45C, SRC) is not built into any product.
3. The medico-legal report pipeline and disbursement funders are not integrated. Reports cost about $4k per case and files run 18–24 months.
4. No AU tool ties the settlement calculation to Australian deductions and the trust ledger.
5. A trust ledger that is both WA-compliant and immutable is a barrier to new entrants. General accounting packages are not compliant.
6. Firms glue together Gmail, Box and Word. Incumbents assume Outlook and their own document management.
7. Lock-in and lack of APIs, especially with LEAP, create an opening for an open API, MCP and full export.
8. No AU client portal is designed for PI.
9. Small firms have no AI intake.

## 5. Architecture trends

- The industry is moving from chat to **supervised agents that read and write the system of record** (Filevine LOIS, Smokeball Archie, Clio Agentic Workflows).
- **Structured, source-linked facts** (Filevine's matter graph, Mary's fact objects) are winning over plain retrieval from stored documents (RAG).
- Firms expect to choose their model and expect MCP. They will want the practice management system exposed as an MCP server.
- Australian buyers increasingly expect Australian hosting and Australian model inference.
- E-sign, client portals and payments are expected as built-in features. AU trust-compliant payments are still patchy.
